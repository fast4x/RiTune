Cast in three steps

RiTune receives, RiPlay sends. There is nothing to configure in between.

  1. 1

    Install RiTune on your TV

    Grab the APK from GitHub Releases — or sideload it on a TV box with AFTVnews Downloader, code 1178386.

  2. 2

    Open RiPlay on your phone

    RiPlay discovers every RiTune receiver on the same Wi-Fi automatically, by name and model.

  3. 3

    Cast and stay in control

    Pick a track and press play. Play, pause and seek from RiPlay while the TV plays it on the big screen.

Everything a receiver needs

A small, focused app that does one job extremely well: be the big screen for your RiPlay library.

Made for Android TV

Registered as a Leanback launcher, with a proper TV banner and D-pad friendly navigation. It also installs normally on phones and tablets.

Zero configuration

RiTune advertises itself on the local network via NSD (_RiPlayLinkApp._tcp). Open RiPlay and your TV is already in the list.

Cast audio & video

An embedded Ktor server on the TV accepts load, play, pause and seek commands from RiPlay over a WebSocket.

Two-way state sync

Playback state is broadcast back to every connected controller in real time: position, play/pause and seeks stay consistent on both screens.

Encrypted local link

The channel runs over HTTPS/WSS with a certificate generated on the device for your TV's own IP. Your stream never leaves the home network.

Open source, forever

100% Kotlin and Jetpack Compose under GPL-3.0, built by the team behind RiPlay. Read it, learn it, improve it.
